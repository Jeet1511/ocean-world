OMNIRUSH — REFERENCE PHOTOS

Purpose
These images are visual references for upgrading the existing underwater world. They are not 3D assets, textures, or permission to copy any third-party artwork.

01_User_Provided_References
The five individual reference images supplied in the conversation. The monumental underwater landscape is the primary reference for large-scale geological composition. The other images guide coral, kelp, shallow-water lighting, and reef scenery.

02_High_Resolution_Environment_Concepts
Six single-frame wide environment concepts for additional composition and atmosphere guidance. Study their large-scale shelves, geological bridges, caves, reef density, deep-water depth, and cinematic lighting. These are conceptual visual guides, not models to use as geometry.

Important art-direction guidance
- Preserve the existing OmniRush project, features, geography, and gameplay. Enhance; do not remove or redesign existing systems.
- Use the large landscape references for geological scale, layered shelves, arches, and distant formations.
- Use the smaller ecosystem references for coral, kelp, fish, and shallow-water details.
- Build actual complete 3D environments with appropriate depth, full geometry, high-quality materials, and deliberate lighting.
- Do not create generic repeated props or disconnected arena scenes.
