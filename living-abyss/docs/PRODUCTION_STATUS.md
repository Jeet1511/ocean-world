It now transforms instance position and applies an appropriate nonuniform-scale normal correction. A real Chromium WebGL render of one rock-material box placed at x=3 produced background at the origin and rock pixels at the instance position (luminance sums 0 versus 14). Triangle surface guards cover 1,234 registered authored geology and large-scree bounds; small decorative rubble remains non-solid. Guards are generated lazily with a 24-entry cache. Twelve sampled real Glass Horizon boulder surfaces registered contact; this is not exhaustive or a guarantee that thin geometry cannot tunnel. Exact collision for cave/other meshes, whole-world routes and performance remain unverified.

The decorative distant seabed was also visibly producing dark triangular wedges in the shallow screenshot: hiding only the apron removed them. Wholly interior apron triangles were removed, and distant apron rendering fades into water rather than drawing hard deep-blue silhouettes over the horizon; boundary/outer apron remains. The seven gate screenshots above show the subsequent result. Edge seams require further QA. See `COLLISION_AUDIT.md` and `tests/apron-regression.mjs`.
