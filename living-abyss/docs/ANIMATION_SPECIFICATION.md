# Animation status

Fish: per-fish local separation, alignment, cohesion, wander, home range, player and analytic-core repulsion are evaluated at 8 fixed steps/s; GPU interpolation fills display frames; a shader tail wave remains. This is a small bounded approximation, not rigging, predictive navigation, or a biological study. Jellyfish: shader bell pulse and ribbon motion. Dolphin: pre-existing animation/controller.

Ten seconds of simulation were tested for centre-point seabed clearance, sea-surface bounds and finite values; no proof of fin/whole-body terrain clearance, roof avoidance or animation quality from every angle. No Blender source or skinned production animals were created in this pass.
