# Underwater exploration: research applied to the existing project

## Sources and observations
- [Giant Squid / Unreal Engine interview on ABZÛ](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/get-to-know-the-ocean-in-journey-like-diving-adventure-abzu): the team discusses shoaling, responsive kelp, independently moving vegetation, artist-directed underwater illumination, and relaxed exploration. Takeaway: animation and interaction should support a readable composed environment, rather than merely increasing object count.
- [GDC: Creating the Art of ABZU](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024409/Creating-the-Art-of-ABZU): discusses authored terrain integrated with objects, dynamic kelp, animated fish, underwater lighting and atmospherics. Takeaway: hero forms, terrain and lighting should be designed together instead of random props scattered over a generic height field.
- [Subnautica art director Cory Strader on flora and biomes](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/subnautica-flora-artwork): describes recognisable silhouettes, real-world biological references, transition zones, fog, clear shallow-water cues and distinct kelp forms. Takeaway: destination identity should come from structure, habitat and light together; use restrained transition spaces rather than copying another game's art.
- [Subnautica kelp-forest concept](https://www.indiedb.com/games/subnautica/news/subnautica-concept-art-kelp-forest): describes thin anchor stems, foliage near the surface, and weaving routes through the forest. Takeaway: kelp should have canopy, layers and traversable clearings.
- [ambientCG Rock 022](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock022): photogrammetry-derived rock material; [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/) says its assets are CC0. Its 1K colour, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are integrated only into the existing limestone/sandstone shader; see `../assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

## Preserve actual current project identities
The user's descriptive terms such as “Sunlit Shelf”, “Drowned Cathedral” and “Crystal Abyss” are art-direction cues, not authorization to rename or move the current project. The seven current named destinations are **The Glass Horizon**, **Roseglass Reef**, **Verdant Cathedral**, **The Pillars of Aurel**, **The Azure Span**, **The Flooded Nave**, and **The Abyssal Bowl**. Coordinates and depth bands remain in `src/world/geography.js`.

## Honest scope of this pass
The actual work in this pass repairs missing rock triangles and tests the reported obstacle area, and introduces a single, licensed rock texture set for the existing material. It does not deliver the complete hybrid art pipeline, new professional animal models, detailed ecosystems, all-destination cinematography, or the visual standard requested. Reference research is not itself proof that those features are complete. Before expanding use of any assets, verify their individual source and license, memory budget, visual quality, and compatibility with current systems.

### Ecosystem increment, 2026-10-31
Giant Squid described animating large fish populations, simulating kelp and deliberately shaping underwater light and authored terrain: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/video-creating-the-striking-underwater-seascapes-of-i-abzu-i-. This pass adds translucent procedural, pulsing jellyfish in two mid-water habitats and licensed photogrammetric shallow-water shell detail; it does not reproduce ABZÛ assets and is not an ABZÛ-level finished ecosystem. Screenshot inspection shows prominent angular rocks and modest jellyfish; more authored geometry, plants, animal assets, lighting and area-by-area QA remain necessary.
