# World-wide ecosystem and rendering pass — in progress

Scope: the existing seven-place game, including its map, portals, controller and dolphin; no replacement scene. This is a work log, **not an AAA-completion certificate**. See [performance](PERFORMANCE_PASS.md), [validation](validation/README.md) and [asset sources](../assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md).

## Implemented and sampled

- Habitat-specific, deterministic supplemental population plans with per-biome minimum/maximum fish budgets, configured school sizes/regions and independently updated groups. The current browser report has 70 school groups and 2,303 fish instances across seven places. This covers fish-school plans, **not** fully configurable population lifecycles for every animal.
- Eight additional locally hosted AI-assisted fish models, a sea-star scan, swaying anemones, existing rigged sharks/rays/whale, and two shallow sea turtles with a Blender-authored five-joint flipper rig. Many school fish still use approximate GPU deformation, not skeletal rigs. The turtles have limited patrols and a simple local swimmer-avoidance offset.
- Six optimized, instanced Smithsonian dry-coral-skeleton scans, recolored as artistic living-reef approximations: 81 planned colonies at Roseglass, 17 at Glass Horizon and 25 deep-coral forms in the Bowl. The flora planner checks sampled terrain and conservative bounds. These placements and museum derivatives are not an ecological census or verified living-species depictions.
- Poly Haven coral/sand maps, a side-offset Roseglass/Aurel approach and lower Roseglass/Bowl arrivals, without moving the existing gates. Reef/deep flora gain true low-poly branch/kelp geometry on low quality while full geometry and placements return on high.
- Flooded Nave's main vault has a physical irregular opening in rendered geometry and its collision mesh. Medium/high use an eight-sample, half-resolution, scene-depth-clipped view-ray single-scattering approximation through the aperture; low uses a cheaper translucent fallback. Smaller object shadows along sunlight and multiple scattering are not simulated.
- All 34 Node regression scripts pass. Chromium/SwiftShader sampled all seven arrivals and a cave interior at low/high with no browser errors/warnings; map, dolphin, pause, seven-choice portal, one Roseglass UI travel and the whale also passed. The independently re-baked turtle normal, two stroke poses and a local avoidance response were inspected in the running world. See the actual screenshots in `validation/`.
- The current performance pass conservatively culls off-screen flora, geology and schools, reuses exact CPU/shader work, and defers three distant assets without changing selected quality. Four matched screenshots were pixel-identical while submitted triangles fell about 21–32% in those views. Initial asset bodies fell about 7.8%. See `PERFORMANCE_PASS.md` for the measurements, added CPU overhead and loading/timeout caveats. No target-device frame-time or thermal benchmark is available.
- Before the current frustum pass, a fixed Roseglass view with the **same instance counts and low shader** submitted approximately 1.64 million flora triangles with low-poly branch/kelp geometry versus 6.65 million with full meshes; the total low scene was approximately 3.76 million triangles before the additional conservative culling. These are renderer submission diagnostics, **not** target-device FPS or temperature measurements.

## Open acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rich, biologically plausible art across every destination comparable to the supplied references. The reef remains patchy, geology is visibly faceted, and deep areas are dark/sparse even after near-camera art-directed fill.
- [ ] Complete species coverage and high-quality anatomy/rigging, including octopuses, squid and crustaceans; species-specific motion, avoidance and habitat use need much more work. Current lionfish, seahorse and other named fish are approximations.
- [ ] Feeding, predation outcomes, migration, reproduction, refuges and a functioning food web. Current predator response is avoidance, not a complete ecosystem.
- [ ] Full continuous swimming and dolphin traversal, portal approaches, cave/rock contact and animal paths; current assertions cover local poses and sampled points only. The terrain still has extreme local slopes.
- [ ] User-machine GPU/CPU frame-time, memory, sustained thermals and quality tuning. Software-WebGL results do not establish device performance.
- [ ] Final production art/animation review, full playthrough and actual AAA sign-off.
