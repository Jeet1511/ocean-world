# Creature and habitat status

The 17 existing schools remain at their established habitat sites and retain 1,420 simple fish; they are not identified, anatomically validated marine species. The current addition is a generic schooling simulation, not species-specific biology. Existing procedural jellyfish have a bell and trailing ribbons but are not rigged or species-verified. Dolphin remains the pre-existing player-mode animal.

NOAA describes kelp habitat as rocky holdfasts, flexible stipes, blades and in some species pneumatocysts, with a canopy, midwater and seafloor community: https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/visit/ecosystems/kelpdesc.html. Species assignment, skeletal models, behaviour profiles and cross-habitat placement audits remain incomplete.
