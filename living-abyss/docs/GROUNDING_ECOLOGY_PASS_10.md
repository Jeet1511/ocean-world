# Update 10 — grounding, cave contact and ecology (in progress)

This is an incremental working build, **not acceptance of the requested whole-world redesign or 30 species**. The seven existing places, their portal topology, player modes and controls remain the existing game. See `PRODUCTION_STATUS.md` and earlier pass records for history.

## Changes
- Lower tower bases are draped/buried against the rendered triangle terrain before collision geometry is registered. Loose rock, flora and cavern root heights use the same rendered-surface sampler. The two limestone formations implicated in image 13 have their first 35 stored vertices below the sampled rendered surface (tested, not a full-world certification). Before and after approximate-pose captures: `../../preservation/visual-audit/grounding-before/heading-3.png` and `../../preservation/visual-audit/grounding-after/heading-3.png`.
- The Flooded Nave and Whispering Vault interior roof curves are smoothed and their rims joined to sampled terrain. Their visible shell has swept sphere surface contact from lateral directions, in addition to the old upward ray. Visible cave spikes are registered for local triangle contact. Open shells use surface-only guards so they do not invent an enclosing solid. Endpoint collision for spike surfaces is not a full swept collision guarantee. Surface sampling, player clearance and gate approach tests are targeted; this is not proof that no cave geometry can be crossed or that no seams remain. Large outer-rim/terrain steps and triangular forms remain.
- Existing named-school count (17) is retained. Added 14 supplemental generic procedural schools with 28 fish each, two per destination, and four animated, textured mixed reef shoals with nine fish each in shallow Glass Horizon/Roseglass. Distance culling and offscreen simulation reduction limit the added cost. Generic forms and the mixed asset are **not 30 verified species**. Fish steering avoids sampled terrain and approximate rock cores, not every cave roof or animated mesh continuously.
- Added 288 fan/sponge-like filter-feeder instances in grouped sites in Pillars of Aurel, Azure Span and Flooded Nave, plus the prior 176 in the deeper bowl. These are generic habitat detail and not identified species. Original locomotion and portal mechanisms remain; assets and their licensing are listed in `assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md` and the in-game chart.

## Research used for habitat direction
- NOAA, [Layers of the Ocean](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/ocean/layers-of-ocean): sunlight zone 0–200 m, mesopelagic 200–1,000 m. The game's named Abyssal Bowl around 560 m is a fictional place name, **not** the real 4,000–6,000 m abyssopelagic zone.
- NOAA Ocean Exploration, [Meet the Deep](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/exploration-extras/24-national-ocean-month/): deep rocky canyons and seamounts support corals, sponges and other animals; place sessile feeders on substrate and do not treat all as photosynthetic tropical corals.
- NOAA, [Deep Sea Coral Communities](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/education/teachers/deep-coral-communities/deep-sea-coral.html): examples span distinct depth ranges; actual distributions vary by species and geography.
- NOAA, [Florida Keys Creature Feature](https://floridakeys.noaa.gov/education/creature-feature.html): sponges, herbivorous fish, morays and turtles use different shallow reef/seagrass niches. This informs future distinct species work; those species are not automatically included in the game.
- Smithsonian, [The Deep Sea](https://ocean.si.edu/ecosystems/deep-sea/deep-sea): light and fauna change by ocean zone; mesopelagic life should not be treated as a bright shallow coral reef.

## Evidence and caveats
- All 20 non-mutating `tests/*.mjs` passed, including `grounding-ecology-pass.mjs` and `cavern-contact-regression.mjs`; `collision-inventory.mjs` is a mutating audit and was excluded. Four short portal approaches per destination passed in the offline audit. These tests are not a continuous full-world playthrough.
- Browser smoke after the collision change passed map open/close, dolphin and pause toggles, a seven-choice portal dialog and one Roseglass arrival. `tools/reef-shoal-smoke.mjs` confirmed four shoal rigs load, nearby playback advances, pause freezes it and distant rigs cull; no captured browser errors/warnings. `tools/marine_smoke.mjs` also passed for the six existing large visitors. The shoal asset's path clearance uses samples and coarse bounds.
- Seven single gate-arrival screenshots in `../../preservation/visual-audit/update10-arrivals/` were recorded with SwiftShader before the new collision-only changes. They show **unfinished** terrain and sparse/dark scenery, particularly at depth; they are not a seven-place quality pass. Low-quality telemetry ranged approximately 156–386 draw calls and 1.73–7.18 million triangles per sampled arrival. Software FPS is not a target-hardware measurement. No target device or end-to-end traversal benchmark was available.
- Missing: thirty actually implemented and identifiable species with researched ranges and behavior; distinct habitat art and playable discoveries in every place; broad animal/roof avoidance; thorough all-angle grounding, collision and navigation; art polish, texture/detail review and target-hardware performance testing. Do not label generic fish or recolors as thirty species to satisfy the count.
