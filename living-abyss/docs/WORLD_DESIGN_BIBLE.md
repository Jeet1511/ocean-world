# World design bible — existing baseline, not final approval

One continuous square ocean chart, seven named places. Keep their positions and depth architecture; user permits redesign but it is not necessary for this pass and existing geography remains the chosen baseline. Preserve existing gate travel. `SEVEN_PLACES.md` contains the authored data, not evidence of visual completion.

1. The Glass Horizon: sunlit shelf, navigable sandy channels.
2. Roseglass Reef: reef limestone and coral terraces with an intended sandy gutter.
3. Verdant Cathedral: kelp forest on rocky foundations with clearings.
4. The Pillars of Aurel: limestone columns and cathedral-scale views.
5. The Azure Span: canyon and natural span with multiple routes.
6. The Flooded Nave: roofed chambers and light openings.
7. The Abyssal Bowl: deep basalt relief and descending basin.

All seven remain **unapproved**: extensive area-specific visual, ecological, and real-navigation evaluation remains.
