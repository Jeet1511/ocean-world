# Reference-led habitat pass 12 — in progress

The user's reference images informed the goals of shallow overhead light, denser coral compositions, fish, kelp and a large whale encounter. They were not copied as game assets. This is a partial art and wildlife pass, not an assertion that the seven places now match the quality of the references.

## What changed
- Composed 30 small garden patches with 407 existing and new foliose coral/fan/sponge forms near the seven established portal approaches. Placed beside, not on, a 30 m central aisle; roots are sampled from the rendered triangular seabed and candidates are checked against conservative geology bounds. Warm branching/table/foliose forms are confined to shallow zones; the deep/sheltered gardens use fan and sponge forms, not a generic tropical reef. Geological bounds and single-point roots are approximate and do not certify every whole footprint or overlapping plant.
- Added a new procedurally constructed layered foliose colony shape, shallow color bounce and denser Roseglass compositions; retained bare-sand passages. Flora instances are spatially tiled and retain quality and distance culling.
- Fixed a shaft shader whose radius calculation suppressed most of its light, positioned light shafts from the sea surface, added a few shallow-route sources and enabled frustum/distance culling. These are stylized additive in-scattering approximations, not true volumetric shadows. Deeper halls do not get an invented surface shaft.
- Integrated one 22 m animated CC BY blue whale in sampled deeper open water near Azure Span; idle/animation and credits are local. Its 24-position route and surrounding terrain/rock samples are conservative, not a guarantee of continuous intersection-free motion, migration, or realistic biology. Its fixed orbit is not complex AI. Credits and checksum: `assets/ASSET_MANIFEST.md` and the in-game chart.
- The existing game, seven locations, gates, player modes, map and prior wildlife remain. Opening/build order now constructs caverns and portals before flora, so the new gardens can respect their approaches. It does not replace the world.

## Research
[NOAA coral distribution](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_corals/coral05_distribution.html) explains the light dependence of most tropical reef-building corals. [NOAA deep coral background](https://www.coris.noaa.gov/activities/resourceCD/resources/deep_coral_bm.pdf) describes cold, non-photosynthetic coral habitats. [NOAA blue whale](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/blue-whale) gives general size and solitary/pair behavior; this encounter is a simplified artistic approximation.
