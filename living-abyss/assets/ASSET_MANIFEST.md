### Textured shallow-school LOD
`models/barramundi_school/school.glb` is a 1,738-triangle static LOD derived from the CC0 Microsoft/Khronos Barramundi Fish above, retaining its base-color texture and UVs. It is generated reproducibly from the packed editable `source/barramundi/barramundi_swim.blend` by `tools/blender/build_barramundi_school_lod.py`. In the three Glass Horizon schools (270 fish), instanced shader tail bending replaces the hero's bone rig; the original steering simulation, counts and locations are retained. These are generic stylized coastal schools using a barramundi model; their schooling behavior is not claimed to be species-accurate. The other 14 schools remain the original untextured procedural fish pending species-appropriate replacements. No other external animal was added in this pass.

Original embedded material textures and models were not copied in full. Approximate triangle counts: surgeonfish 1,536, tuna 1,438 (check actual GLB indices if reprocessed).

### Smithsonian Institution 3D coral specimens (CC0)

The six GLBs below are **modified scans of dry preserved skeletal specimens, not scans of living colored coral**. The runtime normalizes their scale, places repeated instances in a fictional ocean, and applies artistic pigment tints; neither the color, colony size nor abundance is a scientific measurement. Smithsonian suggests attribution and avoiding misrepresentation. We acknowledge Smithsonian Institution, National Museum of Natural History, Invertebrate Zoology. Record names below are the Smithsonian record names and are not asserted to be the current taxonomic names of every in-game instance.

### Rigged sea turtle (CC BY 3.0)

`models/sea_turtle/ocean-turtle.glb` is a derivative of **“Ocean Turtle” by der_On**, [Blend Swap original](https://blendswap.com/blend/8727), [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Original archive SHA-256: `0aad8ad0dd9cfd232588e81b3ab3d36db0a533268cac139de2be5b69d256f1e3`. The original license is in `source/ocean-turtle/BLENDSWAP_LICENSE.txt`; in-game attribution is in the chart credits. The working directory retains the original archive, but the smaller downloadable project package excludes that unmodified 12 MB archive; see the project README for retrieval and verification.

The unrigged original was evaluated at subdivision level 1, converted to modern materials with resized original color/eyelid maps, given position-derived flipper weights and a five-bone armature, and animated with a new approximate synchronized front-paddle stroke in Blender 4.3. The bundled tangent-space normal map was **independently baked from the original archive's object-space normal and mesh**, with `tools/blender/bake_turtle_normal.py`; it does not use the separately distributed derivative normal that was trialed earlier. Baked PNG SHA-256 `a0ffd6e28cb3b7b8e2a84671753879e5010510530a234b8ba11daeb8db420907`. `source/ocean-turtle/ocean_turtle_swim.blend` is the packed editable source; rerig/export with `tools/blender/rig_ocean_turtle.py`. GLB SHA-256 `c1ad1294befcbb95d263e657deaebd8ba72b3873eaea909e18e69772996e7d2a`. A separate [turtle-simulator project](https://github.com/CarpeNoctum101/baby-turtle-simulator-2) was consulted for general reference. Two non-solid turtles follow sampled shallow patrols with simple close-swimmer avoidance. The rig, path and movement have **not** been validated as anatomically accurate or as collision-free continuous routes.
